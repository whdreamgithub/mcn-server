    WHO WE ARE

    About us

    InsMark Influener Agency, founded in 2018 and headquartered in Guangzhou, China, is a global influencer marketing and talent management agency with offices in Shenzhen, London, and New York. We specialize in connecting creators and world-class brands across Asia, Europe, and North America, delivering authentic and impactful campaigns in beauty, tech, lifestyle, and more.

    Since our founding, we have collaborated with over 300 global brands and provided more than $30 million in incentives to creators worldwide.

    At InsMark, we believe in creativity, diversity, and trust as the foundation for meaningful connections between creators and audiences — helping both brands and creators grow together.
