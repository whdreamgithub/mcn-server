Who
We
Are
InsMark is a global influencer marketing agency built for creators. Since 2018, we’ve helped tens of thousands of creators connect with top-tier brands across the US, Europe, and Asia — with no exclusivity, no hidden fees, and always with fast, transparent payments.

When you join InsMark, you get access to real brand opportunities, a personal campaign manager who actually cares, and a team that’s here to help you grow. We believe in creative freedom, mutual respect, and long-term partnerships — because when creators win, we all win.
Step-by-Step: From Deal to Delivery
From brand briefs to viral content — we back you at every step.
1. Brands bring us their campaign brief and budget
We first assess the brand’s professionalism, budget fit, and campaign feasibility before moving forward.
2. We match the right creators to the brief
Our team shortlists creators whose audience and content style align with the campaign needs.
3. You submit your rate and creative direction
We’ll discuss your proposed pricing and content ideas, and make sure they align with brand expectations.
4. Brand reviews and confirms the offer
After alignment, the brand gives final approval based on the proposal and your profile.
5. You decide whether to accept
Interested? Accept. Want to pass? No problem.
6. Sign contract & get your campaign manager onboard
Once confirmed, you’ll sign a project-specific contract with InsMark to align expectations and protect both sides.
Then, your dedicated manager will step in to support execution.
7. You create, we coordinate
You bring the creative — we manage expectations, deadlines, and approvals.
8. You get paid fast after your post goes live
Once your content is published and approved, we guarantee payment within 15–30 days.
No delays, no reminders needed.
