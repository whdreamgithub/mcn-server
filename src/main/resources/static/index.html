    About us

    InsMark Influencer Agency, founded in 2018 and headquartered in Guangzhou, China, is a global influencer marketing and talent management agency with offices in Shenzhen, London, and New York.


    We specialize in connecting creators and world-class brands across Asia, Europe, and North America, delivering authentic and impactful campaigns in beauty, tech, lifestyle, and more.


    Since our founding, we have collaborated with over 300 global brands and provided more than $30 million in incentives to creators worldwide.


    At InsMark, we believe in creativity, diversity, and trust as the foundation for meaningful connections between creators and audiences — helping both brands and creators grow together.

    Frequently Asked Questions
    Which platforms do you work on?
    We run campaigns on TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube, depending on the brand’s needs and your content strengths.
    Can I still work with you if I don’t have a huge following?
    Yes. We care more about your content quality, audience trust, and storytelling style than just numbers.
    Will I get to keep creative control?
    Yes. We always advocate for creator-led storytelling. Brands may share basic guidelines, but your tone, pace, and format matter most.
    How do payments work?
    Once your content is delivered and approved according to the agreement, payment is processed within 15–30 days. No chasing needed.
    How do you choose which creators to work with?
    We look for authentic creators who understand their audience and produce quality content. It's not about fame—it’s about fit.
    Will I know what kind of brand I'm working with in advance?
    Yes. You’ll receive a clear brand brief, including the product type, content expectations, and timeline, before confirming anything.
    Do you offer long-term partnerships?
    Yes. We love building ongoing collaborations with creators we trust. Many of our partnerships go beyond one-off videos.
    Do you require exclusivity?
    Only if the brand specifically requests it—and we’ll always discuss terms transparently with you before you commit.
    Do you help creators grow or just connect them to brands?
    We’re more than a matchmaker. We help creators understand what brands look for, improve briefs together, and position you for future deals.
    How can I join your creator network?
    Just fill out our quick form on the website and include your main socials. Our team will reach out if there's a fit!
    Talk to Us
    Will I sign a contract?
    Yes. For every confirmed campaign, we’ll share a simple agreement that outlines deliverables, deadlines, payment terms, and usage rights. It’s designed to protect both you and the brand.
    What happens if a brand asks for a revision?
    We try to avoid back-and-forth by clarifying expectations upfront. If edits are needed, they must stay within the agreed brief—and we’re here to make sure no unreasonable demands are passed on to you.
